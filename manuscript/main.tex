\begin{abstract}
Extended Wigner's friend scenarios (EWFS) and Local Friendliness (LF) no-go theorems establish that universal unitary quantum dynamics, Local Agency, and the \emph{Absoluteness of Observed Events} (AOE) cannot jointly hold across arbitrary physical scales. Standard formulations of these theorems, however, leave two operational problems unresolved: first, specifying a Lorentz-covariant criterion for when an empirical proposition acquires a definite truth value relative to a localized observer without invoking foliation-dependent state reduction; and second, quantifying how partial environmental record leakage degrades violations across both Bell-type and genuine multi-friend facets of the Local Friendliness polytope. Here we formulate the \emph{Causal Actuality Hypothesis} (CAH), indexing three-valued truth valuations $v_O(P, \tau) \in \{T, F, U\}$ along an observer worldline $x_O(\tau)$ to the local record subalgebra $\mathcal{R}_O(\tau) \subset \mathfrak{A}(J^-(x_O(\tau)))$ inside the observer's causal past light cone, and verify an explicit algebraic gluing condition upon causal horizon intersection. Modeling imperfect laboratory isolation through environmental branch coherence retention $f = |\langle e_+ | e_- \rangle| \in [0, 1]$ and record leakage $\ell = \sqrt{1 - f^2} \in [0, 1]$, we prove via a dimension-independent noncommutative Sum-of-Squares identity in unital $C^*$-algebras that the one-friend Semi-Brukner functional satisfies $B_{\max}^{\mathrm{SB}}(f) = 2\sqrt{1 + f^2} = 2\sqrt{2 - \ell^2}$. Extending the analysis across the five canonical $(3,3,2,2)$ inequality categories tested by Bong \emph{et al.}, we demonstrate a sharp asymptotic separation in the high-leakage regime ($f \to 0, \ell \to 1$): one-friend excess violations decay quadratically as $\Delta S \simeq f^2$, whereas scenarios in which both laboratories leak undergo quartic suppression $\Delta S = \mathcal{O}(f^4)$. For two-qubit systems the CHSH-type functionals obey the product bound $S \le 2\sqrt{1 + f_A^2 f_B^2}$, and both the two-friend Brukner and Bong \emph{et al.}'s genuine LF facets obey the analytical lower bound $\Delta S \ge f^4/(1+f^2)$ (asymptotically exact as $f \to 0$). Finally, we ground the fundamental ceiling on reversible unitary control in soft-graviton and soft-photon decoherence across Killing and cosmological de Sitter horizons following Danielson, Satishchandran, and Wald, closing the flat-spacetime adiabatic reversal loophole in principle while motivating multi-qubit processors as minimalist causal-horizon test beds.
\end{abstract}
