\section{Covariant Horizon Ceilings, Composite Observers, and Open Questions}
\label{sec:dsw_ceilings}

\subsection{Closing the Adiabatic Loophole via Soft-Graviton Horizon Decoherence}

Any attempt to define when a laboratory registration ceases to be unitarily invertible must confront John Bell's objection to FAPP arguments~\cite{bell1990}: if irreversibility reflected merely technological limitations on Wigner's control electronics, $v_O(P, \tau)$ would lack fundamental objectivity. Conversely, invoking ordinary flat-spacetime gravitational wave radiation ($\Gamma_{\mathrm{rad}} \propto G \dddot{Q}^2 / c^5$ for mass quadrupole $Q$) leaves open a well-known \emph{adiabatic reversal loophole}: in horizonless Minkowski spacetime, executing Charlie's registration and Wigner's uncomputation $U_A^\dagger$ sufficiently slowly drives $\dddot{Q} \to 0$, allowing radiative graviton emission into future null infinity to be made arbitrarily small.

Recent breakthroughs in general relativity and algebraic QFT by Danielson, Satishchandran, and Wald (DSW)~\cite{danielson2023,danielson2025} close this adiabatic loophole in principle whenever the ambient spacetime possesses a Killing horizon---including black hole horizons, Rindler acceleration horizons, and the cosmological de Sitter horizon $R_H = \sqrt{3/\Lambda} \approx 1.2\,c / H_0$ that our expanding universe approaches asymptotically. Even when separated branches are opened and recombined arbitrarily slowly, the static Newtonian or Coulomb field difference between spatially displaced record configurations (mass $m$ separated by distance $d$ with quadrupole moment $\sim m d^2$, or charge $q$ with dipole moment $\sim q d$) radiates soft, zero-frequency horizon-crossing quanta over holding proper time $T$, yielding expected entangling soft-quantum numbers~\cite{danielson2023}
\begin{equation}
    \langle N_{\mathrm{GR}} \rangle \sim \frac{G m^2 d^4 T}{\hbar R_H^5}, \qquad \langle N_{\mathrm{EM}} \rangle \sim \frac{q^2 d^2 T}{\hbar \varepsilon_0 R_H^3},
    \label{eq:dsw_flux}
\end{equation}
which suppress branch coherence retention directly as $f \le \exp(-\frac{1}{2}\langle N \rangle)$ ($\ell = \sqrt{1 - f^2} \to 1$ for $\langle N \rangle \gg 1$). Crucially, Eq.~\eqref{eq:dsw_flux} grows \emph{linearly} with duration $T$: slowing Wigner's reversal unitary to suppress radiative multipole derivatives $\dddot{Q}$ lengthens $T$ and increases soft-horizon entanglement.

Writing $R_{\mathrm{acc}}(T) = \langle N_{\mathrm{GR}} \rangle + \langle N_{\mathrm{EM}} \rangle$ for the accumulated number of entangling soft quanta, the condition $R_{\mathrm{acc}} \gtrsim 1$ marks a non-FAPP ceiling beyond which no causal superobserver contained within $R_H$ can recover unit coherence $f \to 1$, however slowly the reversal is run. This ceiling is one of principle only. At cosmological de Sitter scales ($R_H \sim 10^{26}\,\mathrm{m}$), Eq.~\eqref{eq:dsw_flux} gives decoherence times $T_D = T / \langle N \rangle$ exceeding $10^{100}\,\mathrm{s}$ for a $70\,\mathrm{kg}$ friend displaced by $1\,\mathrm{m}$ and exceeding $10^{70}\,\mathrm{s}$ for one elementary charge displaced by $1\,\mathrm{m}$, against a Hubble time of $4 \times 10^{17}\,\mathrm{s}$; DSW make the same observation for the electromagnetic case~\cite{danielson2023}. The decoherence that actually limits $f$ in any laboratory is therefore the ordinary electromagnetic, phononic, and thermal leakage that Bell's objection targets, and Ref.~\cite{danielson2025} shows that horizon-induced decoherence admits an equivalent local description that suitable material bodies can mimic. What the horizon result contributes is not a practical bound but the statement that reversibility fails in principle, rather than merely in practice, once $R_{\mathrm{acc}} \gtrsim 1$.

\subsection{Composite Sub-Horizons and Quantum Processors as Minimalist Observers}

Macroscopic biological observers and room-temperature detectors do not occupy a single idealized worldline; rather, they comprise $\sim 10^{23}$--$10^{27}$ strongly coupled constituent worldlines forming a dense hierarchy of microscopic causal sub-horizons. When a stray photon scatters off peripheral tissue (such as skin rather than a retinal photoreceptor or active avalanche photodiode), a local thermodynamic interaction occurs inside that microscopic worldtube without ever coupling into an addressable cognitive or digital memory register. From Wigner's exterior perspective, however, any unrecovered peripheral scattering drives $\ell \to 1$ ($f \to 0$) just as inexorably as an intentional readout, explaining why macroscopic systems rapidly cross into irreversible classicality without invoking consciousness or non-unitary collapse postulates.

Superconducting and trapped-ion quantum processors overcome this composite-horizon ambiguity by stripping an ``observer'' down to its minimal causal footprint:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Localized Qubit Worldlines and Joint Causal Envelopes:} Each physical qubit occupies a fixed spatial coordinate on a chip with finite signal propagation velocity ($\sim 10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ along on-chip transmission lines). Multi-qubit ``friends'' acquire a well-defined collective record only after entangling two-qubit gates link their past light cones into a shared causal envelope, directly realizing Sec.~\ref{sec:horizons}'s local subalgebra construction.
    \item \textbf{Tunable Leakage Diagnostics:} Coupling friend ancilla qubits weakly to engineered spectator resonators allows experimentalists to dial $f = |\langle e_+ | e_- \rangle|$ continuously across $[0, 1]$, directly testing the predicted transition between $\mathcal{O}(f^2)$ single-friend resilience and $\mathcal{O}(f^4)$ bilateral suppression---more generally, the dependence on the product $f_A f_B$ in Proposition~\ref{prop:bilateral}---across Fig.~\ref{fig:lf_leakage_bounds} and Table~\ref{tab:scaling_comparison}.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Scope and Open Questions}

We conclude by highlighting four open problems suggested by this framework:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Thermodynamic Stabilization Thresholds Within $\mathcal{R}_O(\tau)$:} What minimal redundancy or Landauer free-energy dissipation distinguishes a transient two-qubit entanglement gate from a dynamically stabilized record inside $\mathcal{R}_O(\tau)$ across mesoscopic sub-horizon networks?
    \item \textbf{Infinite-Dimensional SOS Certificates for Genuine LF Facets:} Theorem~1 provides an exact $C^*$-algebraic SOS certificate for Semi-Brukner across all $f \in [0, 1]$, while Proposition~1 provides an analytical lower bound for $S_{\mathrm{GLF}}(f)$. Constructing closed-form Navascu\'{e}s--Pironio--Ac\'{i}n (NPA) SOS certificates for $S_{\max}^{\mathrm{GLF}}(f)$ in general von Neumann algebras remains an open algebraic problem, as does a dimension-independent proof of the two-qubit product bound of Proposition~\ref{prop:bilateral}.
    \item \textbf{Experimental Mapping of the $\mathcal{O}(f^2)$ vs.\ $\mathcal{O}(f^4)$ Window:} Can superconducting or trapped-ion processors operating at two-qubit and mid-circuit readout visibilities $\mu \in [0.88, 0.96]$ experimentally map the intermediate leakage regime where Semi-Brukner and Bell non-LF inequalities are violated while Bong \emph{et al.}'s Genuine LF facet is obeyed?
    \item \textbf{Curved-Spacetime Unruh--DeWitt EWFS Models:} Embedding Fewster--Verch detector couplings~\cite{fewster2020} explicitly into Rindler or Schwarzschild backgrounds to compute exact $f(T, R_H)$ decay curves for two-friend LF facets would unify Sec.~\ref{sec:polytope_bounds}'s algebraic bounds with Sec.~\ref{sec:dsw_ceilings}'s horizon decoherence rates from first principles.
\end{enumerate}
