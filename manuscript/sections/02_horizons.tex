\section{Record Horizons: Causal and Laboratory}
\label{sec:horizons}

This section states the bookkeeping that motivates the leakage parameter of Sec.~\ref{sec:polytope_bounds}. It introduces no dynamics beyond standard relativistic quantum theory, and Secs.~\ref{sec:polytope_bounds} and~\ref{sec:noise_budget} can be read without it.

Let $(\mathcal{M}, g)$ be a time-oriented globally hyperbolic spacetime, with $J^+(x)$ and $J^-(x)$ the causal future and past of a point $x$. Following algebraic quantum field theory and the Fewster--Verch measurement framework~\cite{fewster2020,alves2024}, each precompact causally convex region $\mathcal{O} \subset \mathcal{M}$ carries a unital $C^*$-algebra $\mathfrak{A}(\mathcal{O})$, with $\mathfrak{A}(\mathcal{O}_1) \subset \mathfrak{A}(\mathcal{O}_2)$ whenever $\mathcal{O}_1 \subset \mathcal{O}_2$, and the algebras of spacelike-separated regions commute. An observer $O$ is a localized apparatus or agent on a future-directed timelike worldline $x_O(\tau)$. The boundary $\partial J^-(x_O(\tau))$ is the \emph{causal record horizon} of $O$ at proper time $\tau$: nothing outside $J^-(x_O(\tau))$ can have influenced $O$ by then. Inside $\mathfrak{A}(J^-(x_O(\tau)))$ we single out the commutative \emph{record subalgebra} $\mathcal{R}_O(\tau)$ generated by the orthogonal projections $\{\Pi_P\}$ onto dynamically stabilized, addressable memory states carried by $O$. If a superobserver coherently reverses an interaction before $\tau$, so that the memory register returns to a fiducial product state, the corresponding projection is not in $\mathcal{R}_O(\tau)$.

\begin{definition}[Causal Actuality Hypothesis]
Let $P$ be an empirical proposition associated with a local measurement interaction occurring in spacetime region $\mathcal{O}_P \subset \mathcal{M}$, represented on a stabilized memory register by projection $\Pi_P \in \mathcal{R}_O(\tau)$. Relative to observer $O$ at proper time $\tau$ in global algebraic state $\omega$, the truth valuation $v_O(P, \tau) \in \{T, F, U\}$ is defined by
\begin{equation}
    v_O(P, \tau) =
    \begin{cases}
        T, & \text{if } \mathcal{O}_P \subset J^-(x_O(\tau)), \; \Pi_P \in \mathcal{R}_O(\tau), \\
           & \text{and } \omega_O(\Pi_P) = 1, \\
        F, & \text{if } \mathcal{O}_P \subset J^-(x_O(\tau)), \; \Pi_P \in \mathcal{R}_O(\tau), \\
           & \text{and } \omega_O(\Pi_P) = 0, \\
        U, & \text{otherwise,}
    \end{cases}
    \label{eq:cah_valuation}
\end{equation}
where $\omega_O$ denotes the state conditioned on the classical record configuration registered along $O$'s worldtube up to $\tau$, and $U$ denotes objective causal indeterminacy relative to $\mathcal{R}_O(\tau)$ rather than mere epistemic ignorance of a pre-existing value.
\end{definition}

Three remarks fix the content of Definition~1. First, it is bookkeeping. An event in a spacelike-separated region has valuation $U$ for $O$, and microcausality already guarantees that expectation values on $\mathfrak{A}(\mathcal{O}_A)$ are unaffected by unitary or non-selective operations in a spacelike-separated region $\mathcal{O}_B$ (a selective operation, which conditions on an outcome obtained in $\mathcal{O}_B$, does change conditional expectations for correlated states). CAH adds only the rule that no truth value is assigned across a spacelike hypersurface, in line with the past-light-cone reduction rule of Hellwig and Kraus~\cite{hellwig1970}. Three-valued valuations go back to Reichenbach~\cite{reichenbach1944}; here $\{T, F, U\}$ records membership in local record algebras and leaves classical logic on shared records untouched. Second, because $\omega_O$ is conditioned on the registered record configuration, every projection in $\mathcal{R}_O(\tau)$ takes the value $0$ or $1$ in $\omega_O$, and any record carried by $O$ registers an interaction inside $J^-(x_O(\tau))$. The operative condition is therefore membership, $\Pi_P \in \mathcal{R}_O(\tau)$, that is, what stabilizes a memory state; graded valuations for partial leakage $0 < f < 1$ are left for future work. Third, two boundaries must be kept apart. The causal record horizon enforces $v_{O_A}(P_B, \tau_A) = U$ at spacelike separation. In a Wigner's-friend scenario, by contrast, the friend's interaction lies \emph{inside} the superobserver's causal past, and $v_{\bar{W}}(P_r, \tau) = U$ reflects coherent isolation across what we term the \emph{laboratory horizon}: the boundary of the degrees of freedom that the superobserver can still reverse. The leakage $\ell$ of Eq.~\eqref{eq:polarity_convention} measures how far a record has crossed it, and the remainder of the paper concerns that boundary.

\paragraph{Consistency when records meet.}
A record-relative valuation must remain consistent when two initially spacelike-separated observers $O_A$ and $O_B$ compare records in their joint causal future. Let $O_A$ register a dichotomic observable $M_A = \Pi_A^{(+)} - \Pi_A^{(-)} \in \mathfrak{A}(\mathcal{O}_A)$ through a local isometry $V_A$ into a memory $R_A$, and let $O_B$ register $M_B \in \mathfrak{A}(\mathcal{O}_B)$ through $V_B$ into $R_B$; spacelike separation gives $[V_A, V_B] = 0$. A classical copy of a record, modeled by a controlled-copy isometry that is diagonal in the record basis, cannot disagree with its source, and the memory observables $\Sigma_{R} = \Pi_{R}^{(+)} - \Pi_{R}^{(-)}$ satisfy
\begin{equation}
    \omega\bigl(V_B^* V_A^* (\Sigma_{R_A} \otimes \Sigma_{R_B}) V_A V_B\bigr) = \omega(M_A M_B),
    \label{eq:gluing_corr}
\end{equation}
so that comparison in the joint future reproduces the quantum joint distribution, Bell correlations up to $2\sqrt{2}$ included. Neither property is an additional postulate. Equation~\eqref{eq:gluing_corr} is the relational account of EPR correlations of Smerlak and Rovelli~\cite{smerlak2007} in algebraic form, a consistency check that unitary dynamics passes automatically. CAH refines Relational Quantum Mechanics~\cite{rovelli1996,dibiagio2021} only in tying relative facts to record algebras inside causal horizons, and it is complementary to Quantum Darwinism analyses of the friend's record~\cite{zurek2018,rivlin2025}.

\paragraph{Inference across an uncomputed horizon.}
The Frauchiger--Renner argument~\cite{frauchiger2018} derives a contradiction from universal unitary validity~$(\mathrm{Q})$, single outcomes~$(\mathrm{S})$, and cross-agent consistency~$(\mathrm{C})$, where $(\mathrm{C})$ lets a superobserver~$\bar{W}$ adopt the certainty of an encapsulated agent~$\bar{F}$ even though $\bar{W}$ goes on to perform an interference measurement on $\bar{F}$'s laboratory. Under CAH, consistency between observers is required only for records that persist into a common future. $\bar{W}$'s measurement erases $\mathcal{R}_{\bar{F}}$ instead of copying it, so $\bar{F}$'s proposition remains $U$ relative to $\bar{W}$ and cannot anchor the inference. With leakage, the coherence on which $\bar{W}$'s measurement relies is reduced by the factor~$f$, the deterministic Born-rule step invoked under~$(\mathrm{Q})$ holds only approximately, and a stabilized record of $\bar{F}$'s outcome exists outside the laboratory only at $\ell = 1$. This is a reading of the argument in the present terms, not a new resolution of it. It leaves the correlation-only Local Friendliness inequalities, which need no assumption about reasoning, as the operational test, and we turn to them now.
