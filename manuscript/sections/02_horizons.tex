\section{Covariant Record Horizons and Causal Bookkeeping}
\label{sec:horizons}

\subsection{Local Algebras, Record Horizons, and Truth Valuations}

Let $(\mathcal{M}, g)$ be a time-oriented globally hyperbolic spacetime. For any point $x \in \mathcal{M}$, let $J^-(x)$ and $J^+(x)$ denote its causal past and causal future, respectively. Following algebraic quantum field theory and the Fewster--Verch measurement framework~\cite{fewster2020,alves2024}, we associate to each precompact causally convex region $\mathcal{O} \subset \mathcal{M}$ a unital $C^*$-algebra $\mathfrak{A}(\mathcal{O})$ satisfying isotony ($\mathcal{O}_1 \subset \mathcal{O}_2 \implies \mathfrak{A}(\mathcal{O}_1) \subset \mathfrak{A}(\mathcal{O}_2)$) and Einstein microcausality:
\begin{equation}
    [A, B] = 0 \quad \forall A \in \mathfrak{A}(\mathcal{O}_A), \; B \in \mathfrak{A}(\mathcal{O}_B)
    \label{eq:microcausality}
\end{equation}
whenever $\mathcal{O}_A$ and $\mathcal{O}_B$ are spacelike separated.

Consider a physical observer $O$ modeled as a localized apparatus or agent sweeping out a future-directed timelike worldtube centered on worldline $x_O(\tau)$ parameterized by proper time $\tau$. The boundary $\partial J^-(x_O(\tau))$ defines the \emph{covariant record horizon} of $O$ at proper time $\tau$: no physical signal or dynamical coupling originating outside $J^-(x_O(\tau))$ can influence $O$'s accessible degrees of freedom at or before $\tau$. Within $\mathfrak{A}(J^-(x_O(\tau)))$, we distinguish the commutative \emph{record subalgebra} $\mathcal{R}_O(\tau)$ generated by orthogonal projections $\{\Pi_P\}$ corresponding to dynamically stabilized, addressable memory states carried along $O$'s worldtube at proper time $\tau$. Crucially, if a superobserver coherently reverses a prior interaction before $\tau$ so that the corresponding memory register returns to a product fiducial state, the transient projection is removed from $\mathcal{R}_O(\tau)$.

\begin{definition}[Causal Actuality Hypothesis]
Let $P$ be an empirical proposition associated with a local measurement interaction occurring in spacetime region $\mathcal{O}_P \subset \mathcal{M}$, represented on a stabilized memory register by projection $\Pi_P \in \mathcal{R}_O(\tau)$. Relative to observer $O$ at proper time $\tau$ in global algebraic state $\omega$, the truth valuation $v_O(P, \tau) \in \{T, F, U\}$ is defined by
\begin{equation}
    v_O(P, \tau) =
    \begin{cases}
        T, & \text{if } \mathcal{O}_P \subset J^-(x_O(\tau)), \; \Pi_P \in \mathcal{R}_O(\tau), \\
           & \text{and } \omega_O(\Pi_P) = 1, \\
        F, & \text{if } \mathcal{O}_P \subset J^-(x_O(\tau)), \; \Pi_P \in \mathcal{R}_O(\tau), \\
           & \text{and } \omega_O(\Pi_P) = 0, \\
        U, & \text{otherwise,}
    \end{cases}
    \label{eq:cah_valuation}
\end{equation}
where $\omega_O$ denotes the state conditioned on the classical record configuration registered along $O$'s worldtube up to $\tau$, and $U$ denotes objective causal indeterminacy relative to $\mathcal{R}_O(\tau)$ rather than mere epistemic ignorance of a pre-existing value.
\end{definition}

Under Eq.~\eqref{eq:cah_valuation}, single-system superpositions prior to registration and measurement events occurring in a spacelike-separated region $\mathcal{O}_B$ ($\mathcal{O}_B \cap J^-(x_{O_A}(\tau_A)) = \emptyset$) both satisfy $v_{O_A}(P_B, \tau_A) = U$. Microcausality~\eqref{eq:microcausality} already ensures in standard relativistic quantum theory that local expectation values on $\mathfrak{A}(\mathcal{O}_A)$ are invariant under unitary or selective operations in $\mathcal{O}_B$; CAH elevates this operational invariance into an explicit bookkeeping rule by dispensing with global truth assignments across spacelike hypersurfaces altogether.

\subsection{Record-Gluing Consistency in Overlapping Future Light Cones}

Any relational or horizon-indexed valuation must guarantee intersubjective consistency when two initially spacelike-separated observers $O_A$ and $O_B$ transmit classical signals into their joint causal future $\mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{meet}} \subset J^+(x_A) \cap J^+(x_B)$. Suppose $O_A$ measures a local dichotomic observable $M_A = \Pi_A^{(+)} - \Pi_A^{(-)} \in \mathfrak{A}(\mathcal{O}_A)$ via a local isometric registration coupling $V_A$ into memory register $R_A$, and $O_B$ similarly registers $M_B = \Pi_B^{(+)} - \Pi_B^{(-)} \in \mathfrak{A}(\mathcal{O}_B)$ via $V_B$ into $R_B$. Because $\mathcal{O}_A$ and $\mathcal{O}_B$ are spacelike separated, $[V_A, V_B] = 0$.

When records from $R_A$ and $R_B$ propagate causally into $\mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{meet}}$ without coherent reversal, both record projections enter the joint commutative subalgebra $\mathcal{R}_{O_A}(\tau_{\mathrm{meet}}) \vee \mathcal{R}_{O_B}(\tau_{\mathrm{meet}}) \subset \mathfrak{A}(J^-(x_{\mathrm{meet}}))$. Consistency of record comparison is then governed by two algebraic properties of the underlying state $\omega$:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Faithful Record Copying:} If $O_A$'s stabilized record projection $\Pi_{R_A}^{(a)}$ ($a \in \{+, -\}$) is communicated to $O_B$'s receiving register $\tilde{R}_A$ inside $\mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{meet}}$ via a faithful classical channel (modeled by a controlled-copy isometry $W_{R_A \to \tilde{R}_A}$ diagonal in the record basis), the disagreement operator $\Delta_A = \Pi_{R_A}^{(+)} \Pi_{\tilde{R}_A}^{(-)} + \Pi_{R_A}^{(-)} \Pi_{\tilde{R}_A}^{(+)}$ satisfies $\omega(\Delta_A) = 0$ identically. Neither observer ever encounters contradictory records upon horizon merger.
    \item \textbf{Preservation of Entangled Joint Correlations:} Because $[V_A, V_B] = 0$, the joint expectation of the registered memory observables inside $\mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{meet}}$ satisfies
    \begin{equation}
        \omega\bigl(V_B^* V_A^* (\Sigma_{R_A} \otimes \Sigma_{R_B}) V_A V_B\bigr) = \omega(M_A M_B),
        \label{eq:gluing_corr}
    \end{equation}
    where $\Sigma_{R_A} = \Pi_{R_A}^{(+)} - \Pi_{R_A}^{(-)}$. Consequently, while $v_{O_A}(P_B, \tau_A) = U$ prior to light-cone intersection, local comparison inside $\mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{meet}}$ reproduces the exact quantum joint distribution---including Tsirelson-level Bell correlations up to $2\sqrt{2}$---purely from unitary propagation of local records into the shared causal past of the comparison event.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Exclusion of cross-horizon inference chaining.}
This record-gluing criterion also resolves epistemic self-reference paradoxes such as the Frauchiger--Renner thought experiment~\cite{frauchiger2018}, which derives a contradiction from universal unitary validity~$(\mathrm{Q})$, single outcomes~$(\mathrm{S})$, and cross-agent consistency~$(\mathrm{C})$. Assumption~$(\mathrm{C})$ requires an external superobserver~$\bar{W}$ to promote an encapsulated agent~$\bar{F}$'s internal certainty into $\bar{W}$'s own reasoning chain even when $\bar{W}$ subsequently executes a complementary interference measurement on $\bar{F}$'s isolated laboratory ($\ell = 0$, $f = 1$). Under the Causal Actuality Hypothesis, transitive consistency across observers holds only when physical records persist into an overlapping future light cone $J^+(x_{\bar{F}}) \cap J^+(x_{\bar{W}})$ to form a joint record algebra $\mathcal{R}_{\bar{F}} \vee \mathcal{R}_{\bar{W}}$. Because $\bar{W}$'s complementary projection unitarily rotates and erases $\mathcal{R}_{\bar{F}}$ rather than extracting a classical record across the laboratory horizon, $\bar{F}$'s intermediate proposition remains strictly indeterminate ($v_{\bar{W}}(P_r, \tau) = U$) relative to $\bar{W}$ and cannot anchor modal inference chaining across an uncomputed horizon. Conversely, if partial distinguishability $\ell = \sqrt{1 - f^2} > 0$ leaks across the horizon so that $P_r$ does actualize inside $\mathcal{R}_{\bar{W}}$, the off-diagonal coherence supporting $\bar{W}$'s interference projection decays continuously by factor~$f$, revoking the deterministic Born-rule step invoked under~$(\mathrm{Q})$. Blocking cross-horizon inference chaining dissolves epistemic Wigner's-friend paradoxes without modifying unitary dynamics, leaving correlation-only Local Friendliness inequalities---which require no modal reasoning axioms---as the decisive operational test of record horizons.
